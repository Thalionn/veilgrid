VeilGrid 2.3.18 — first fix after launch and delivery counters.

- Messages now retry for a few seconds while the Veilid node is attaching, so
  the first location after opening the app (or after a restart) is no longer
  dropped with "offline".
- The per-minute diagnostics line now shows `tx` (age and count of the last
  successful send) and `rx` (age and count of the last received fix), plus a
  `PAUSE` entry whenever sharing pauses or resumes and why.
