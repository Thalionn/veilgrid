VeilGrid 2.3.16 — reliable linking and last known positions.

- Two-way linking now retries until the inviter answers with a profile,
  including across parked windows and app restarts, so a link is no longer lost
  when the other phone was detached at that moment.
- Adding a contact no longer fails when the route cannot be resolved right
  away; the contact is saved and the route is looked up again on the next send.
- Contact positions are persisted, so the last known marker stays on the map
  after a restart until a fresh fix arrives.
- Battery diagnostics gains detailed LINK, LOC and MSG entries: link requests
  accepted/rejected with the reason, route resolution, retries, profile
  exchange, and dropped messages.
