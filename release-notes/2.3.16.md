VeilGrid 2.3.16 — more reliable linking and last known positions.

- Two-way linking now retries the link request for up to two hours, including
  across parked windows and app restarts, until the inviter answers with a
  profile or a location, so an invite is not lost just because the other phone
  was detached when it arrived.
- Adding a contact no longer fails when the route cannot be resolved right
  away; the contact is saved and the route is looked up again on the next send.
- Contact positions are saved about every 30 seconds, so the last known marker
  stays on the map after a restart until a fresh fix arrives.
- Battery diagnostics gains detailed LINK, LOC and MSG entries: link requests
  accepted or rejected (with the reason), route resolution, retries, profile
  exchange, and dropped messages.
