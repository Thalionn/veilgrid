VeilGrid 2.3.7 — live driving, calmer tracking, true-size place circles.

- Tracking now adapts to motion:
  - in Battery saver and Balanced, a stationary phone drops to the low-power
    tier instead of polling at high accuracy every minute;
  - while driving, updates speed up (about every 5–10 s) so contacts can see
    the car move along the road, then slow back down once you have stopped.
- Driving is detected from both Activity Recognition and GPS speed, so live
  updates still work on phones where Activity Recognition is unavailable.
- A custom update rate no longer slows driving updates; it applies while you
  are resting or on foot.
- Contact details now show a state line: “Driving”, “At Work · 2h 15m” or
  “Stationary · 40m”, so you can tell where someone is and how long they have
  been there without watching a live dot.
- Place and home radius circles are now drawn as real map polygons rather than
  pixel-sized circles: they cover their true ground area and grow and shrink
  correctly as you zoom.
- Location history/trails were removed. They wrote location points to a local
  database and rebuilt every stored trail whenever a location arrived, and we
  found little daily value in them.
