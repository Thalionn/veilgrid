VeilGrid 2.3.8 — contact context, editable places, Activity, units and speed.

- Contact rows in the Contacts tab and the map's contact sheet now show where
  someone is at a glance: “At Work · 2h 15m”, “Driving” or “Stationary · 40m”.
  Tap a contact for battery, distance, last seen and sharing controls; dwell
  time survives app restarts for place stays.
- Saved places can be edited: tap a place to rename it, change its radius, or
  move it to your current position. The same edit action is available from the
  map's place sheet.
- Activity is now its own tab, holding place arrivals/departures and movement.
  Filter by one or more contacts, or by arrivals, departures and movement;
  entries persist across restarts and tapping one opens that contact.
- Contacts now show their phone battery in the detail sheet, with a charging
  indicator and a red level when it is low.
- Saved places produce arrival and departure alerts (“Alex arrived at Work”,
  “Alex left Work”). The first fix from each contact after opening the app only
  establishes state, so it will not replay arrivals for everyone already at a
  place, and a 40 m exit margin stops GPS jitter from firing leave/arrive pairs
  at the edge.
- Settings → Units switches distances in the whole app between miles (ft, mi)
  and kilometers (m, km): contact distances, place and home radii, and the
  battery update distance.
- Faster startup and smoother navigation:
  - tabs are built when first opened instead of all at launch;
  - the saved theme and unit choice are applied before the first frame;
  - Geist fonts are bundled, so text no longer swaps in after a download;
  - the map style is no longer re-encoded, so theme and data-saver switches are
    faster;
  - sliders save when you release them instead of on every movement.
- The saved theme now persists across restarts (it was not loaded at boot).
- Fixed an error at startup when a saved home radius had been stored as a whole
  number by an older build; it is now read as a number of either type.
