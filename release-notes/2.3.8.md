VeilGrid 2.3.8 — contact battery, place alerts, units and a faster app.

- Contacts now show their phone battery in the contact sheet, with a charging
  indicator and a red level when it is low. Battery is sent alongside the
  location update, so it stays roughly as fresh as the last seen time.
- Saved places now produce arrival and departure alerts: “Alex arrived at
  Work” or “Alex left Work” in the Places tab, next to movement alerts. The
  first fix from each contact after opening the app only establishes state, so
  it will not replay arrivals for everyone already at a place, and a 40 m exit
  margin stops GPS jitter from firing leave/arrive pairs at the edge.
- Settings → Units now switches the whole app between miles (ft, mi, mph) and
  kilometers (m, km, km/h): contact distances, place and home radii, the
  battery update distance, and speeds.
- Faster startup and smoother navigation:
  - tabs are built when first opened instead of all four at launch;
  - the saved theme and unit choice are applied before the first frame;
  - Geist fonts are bundled, so text no longer swaps in after a download;
  - the map style is cached, so theme and data-saver switches are immediate;
  - sliders save when you release them instead of on every movement.
- The saved theme now persists across restarts (it was not loaded at boot).
- Fixed an error at startup when a saved home radius had been stored as a whole
  number by an older build; it is now read as a number of either type.
