VeilGrid 2.3.8 — contact battery and place alerts.

- Contacts now show their phone battery in the contact sheet, with a charging
  indicator and a red level when it is low. Battery is sent alongside the
  location update, so it stays roughly as fresh as the last seen time.
- Saved places now produce arrival and departure alerts: “Alex arrived at
  Work” or “Alex left Work” in the Places tab, next to movement alerts. The
  first fix after opening the app only establishes state, so it will not
  replay arrivals for everyone already at a place, and a 40 m exit margin
  stops GPS jitter from firing leave/arrive pairs at the edge.
- Fixed a startup failure when a saved home radius had been stored as a whole
  number by an older build; it is now read as a number of either type.
