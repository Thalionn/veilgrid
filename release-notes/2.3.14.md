VeilGrid 2.3.14 — battery follow-up.

- Further stationary tracking hardening: a one-minute timer forces the
  low-power tier after three minutes without movement even when no new fix
  arrives, and the tracker reads back the native preset so Battery diagnostics
  shows what is actually active.
- Removed the geolocator plugin and its background service; distance
  calculations now use a local haversine helper.
- Clean 8-minute stationary screen-off test: 0.97 mAh total, with no GPS and
  no Wi-Fi activity.
