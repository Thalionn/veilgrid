VeilGrid 2.3.17 — auto-pause at home no longer gets stuck.

While paused at home the tracker runs on the passive tier and can receive no
fixes at all, so leaving home was not noticed and sharing could stay paused
for a whole trip. Now:

- Movement activity (walking or driving) triggers a single low-accuracy fix to
  check whether you have left the home radius.
- While paused at home, a low-accuracy fix is also taken every 5 minutes as a
  fallback, so leaving home resumes sharing within a few minutes even if
  activity detection stays quiet.
- Home enter/exit is logged with the distance, and the per-minute diagnostics
  line now shows the pause reason (home, incognito or battery).
