VeilGrid 2.3.11 — Home grouping, stationary GPS fix and diagnostics.

- Your Home location is now treated as a map spot with the same icon marker
  as a place.
- When Home is the nearest spot and you or a contact is within about 100 ft,
  those avatars collapse into one home marker instead of separate markers.
- The Home sheet lists “You” when you are there.
- Stationary tracking now forces the low-power tier after three minutes
  without 30 m of movement, even when Activity Recognition is stuck. This
  fixes GPS staying active while the phone sits still.
- New Settings → Battery & tracking → **Battery diagnostics**: a live,
  on-device log of activity, preset changes, GPS fixes, sends and low-battery
  pauses, with copy/share actions.
