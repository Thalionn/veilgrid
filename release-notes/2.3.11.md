VeilGrid 2.3.11 — Home as a map spot and cleaner marker grouping.

- Your Home location is now a map spot with the same icon marker as a place.
  When you or a contact is within 100 ft of Home, those avatars collapse into
  one home marker instead of separate dots.
- Place and person markers now render at the same size; profile photos are
  scaled to match initials badges and place icons.
- Places with people inside use one grouped marker with avatar badges and a
  “+N” count instead of overlapping separate markers.
- Place sheets and the Places tab include “You” when you are inside a spot.
