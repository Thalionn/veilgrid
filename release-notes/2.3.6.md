VeilGrid 2.3.6 — battery and map data controls.

- New Settings → Battery & tracking menu:
  - choose Battery saver / Balanced / Live tracking;
  - set a custom update interval (30–600 s) and minimum distance (25–1000 m);
  - pause sharing automatically below a chosen battery level (10/15/20/30%).
- New "Wi-Fi only basemap" option: on mobile data the map hides its tiles —
  contacts, trails, places and your own position still show — with a banner to
  change it. A moving map can otherwise download hundreds of megabytes in a
  long trip.
- Basemap tiles now use a 512 MB persistent cache, so panning back over an
  area and later sessions reuse tiles instead of re-downloading them.
