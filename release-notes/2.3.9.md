VeilGrid 2.3.9 — quick invites, two-way linking, profile photos and map display.

- Contacts screen: removed the duplicate add-contact button, and replaced the
  tiny copy icon with **Copy code** and **Share** buttons. Share opens the
  system share sheet with an invite link for any messaging app.
- **Two-way link** option: turn it on before sharing. After the other person
  adds you from the link, they are linked back automatically, so neither of you
  needs to scan a second QR code. The link carries a secret that can be revoked
  by turning two-way off.
- **Profile photos**: choose or remove a picture in Settings → Profile. Photos
  are sent directly to your contacts, shown in contact rows, and used as the
  avatar over your map marker.
- **Map display** settings: hide radius circles, hide all saved places, or pick
  a default place color. Each place can also be hidden or given its own color
  from its edit sheet.
- Invite links open a small page that shows the code to copy; contact photos
  and two-way requests work between 2.3.9+ clients.
