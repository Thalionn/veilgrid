VeilGrid 2.3.9 — quick invites, two-way linking, profile photos and map display.

- Contacts screen: removed the duplicate add-contact button from the top bar,
  and there are now **Copy code** and **Share** buttons under your address.
  Share opens the system share sheet with an invite link for any messaging app.
- **Two-way link** option: turn it on before tapping **Share** (the QR code and
  **Copy code** stay one-way). After the other person adds you from the link,
  they are linked back to you automatically, so neither of you needs to scan a
  second QR code. The link carries a secret; turning two-way off again
  disables it until you turn two-way back on.
- **Profile photos**: choose or remove a picture in Settings → Profile. When
  you pick one it is sent directly to your contacts, shown in contact rows, and
  used as your marker on their maps. Removing it clears it on your device only;
  contacts keep the last photo they received.
- **Map display** settings: hide radius circles, hide saved places on the map
  without deleting them, or pick a default place color. Each place can also be
  hidden or given its own color when you edit it.
- Editing a place now includes the same address search as adding one, so an
  existing place can be moved to a searched address.
- Contact names refresh automatically: a contact's newly published display name
  replaces the old one unless you have set a nickname for them, which always
  wins. Names already on your device before this update are kept as they are.
- Invite links open a small page that shows the code to copy; contact photos
  and two-way linking work between 2.3.9+ clients.
