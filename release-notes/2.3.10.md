VeilGrid 2.3.10 — bigger, clearer map markers.

- Contacts without a profile photo now get a colored circular badge with up to
  three letters or digits from their name, instead of a small dot.
- Your own marker gets the same treatment: your photo, initials, or “YOU” when
  no name is set.
- All markers are larger, including profile photos, so they are easier to read
  and tap on the map.
