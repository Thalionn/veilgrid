VeilGrid 2.3.10 — bigger markers and place icons.

- Contact and self markers are four times their previous area (twice the
  diameter), so photos, initials and names are easier to read and tap.
- Contacts without a profile photo show a colored circular badge with up to
  three letters or digits from their name; your own marker uses your photo,
  initials, or “YOU” when no name is set.
- Saved places can be given an icon — home, work, school, gym, food, medical,
  park and more. Place icons render on the map at the same size as people
  markers.
