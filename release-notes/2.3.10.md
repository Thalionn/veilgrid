VeilGrid 2.3.10 — bigger markers, place icons and grouping.

- Contact and self markers are much larger than before, so photos and initials
  are easier to read and tap.
- Contacts without a profile photo show a colored circular badge with up to
  three letters or digits from their name; your own marker uses your photo,
  initials, or “YOU” when no name is set.
- Saved places can be given an icon — home, work, school, gym, food, medical,
  park and more. Place icons render at the same size as person markers.
- When you or a contact is inside a saved place, the place marker shows the
  matching avatars grouped with its icon instead of separate overlapping
  markers. The place sheet lists who is there.
- Your Home location is treated as a map spot too: when you and contacts are
  there, the home marker shows the matching avatars instead of separate dots.
