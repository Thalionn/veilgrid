VeilGrid 2.3.12 — battery diagnostics and stationary tracking fix.

- New Settings → Battery & tracking → **Battery diagnostics**: a live,
  on-device log of activity, tracking preset changes, GPS fixes, sends and
  low-battery pauses, with copy/share actions.
- Stationary tracking now forces the low-power tier after three minutes
  without 30 m of movement, even when Activity Recognition is stuck on
  walking. Driving still gets live updates. This stops VeilGrid from keeping
  GPS active while the phone sits still.
