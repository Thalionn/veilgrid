VeilGrid 2.3.3 — map and home-zone fixes.

- The map now opens on your last known location instead of a placeholder,
  and asks for a fresh fix when the last update is stale.
- Startup is cleaner: the last fix is loaded before the first frame and the
  launch splash is dark instead of flashing white.
- Auto-pause at home now works, including when you are already inside the
  radius. It is computed from the location stream with hysteresis instead of
  relying on the location plugin's OS geofence events, which never arrive on
  Android.
- The home radius is adjustable (50–1000 m) in Settings, and applies
  immediately.
