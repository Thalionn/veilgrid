VeilGrid 2.3.5 — battery controls.

- New Settings → Battery & tracking menu:
  - choose Battery saver, Balanced or Live tracking, with a description of
    each;
  - set a custom update interval (30–600 s) and minimum distance (25–1000 m);
  - pause sharing automatically below a chosen battery level (10/15/20/30%).
- Low-battery pause stops sending and drops to the battery-saving tracking
  preset; it resumes when charging or once the battery recovers a few points.
